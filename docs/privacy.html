Privacy Policy

Robloxian is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own video files to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) to upload videos. It does not read, list or change any other data in the YouTube account.

Data collection and sharing

Robloxian does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share personal data. The login token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to anyone other than YouTube/Google, in order to upload the owner's videos.
